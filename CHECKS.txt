PLAY CHECKS OWED  (28 checks, 6 loads, about 60 minutes)

Answer on the line that starts with ">". Leave it blank if you didn't try that one.
Write whatever you like: "yes", "no, it did X", "didn't try". Prose is fine.
Cheats (debug builds): on the board [1] adds $500, [2] adds 20 rep, [4] takes 20 rep off,
[3] skips to the week's end (payday); in a job [0] finishes it perfectly.


================================================================================
LOAD 1 of 3: A JOB ON FOOT      (about 14 min, a garden with a dog and a hose is best)
================================================================================

S9-ARC
Pick up a stone and HOLD throw. Up/down tilt the throw: a little white sight by your hand
tilts and the ring moves. Let go: the stone flies in an arc, its shadow along the ground,
and lands on the ring. Does the tilt speed feel right?
>

S9-LOB
Throw low at the side fence: it bounces back into the garden. Lob it high: it clears the
fence and is gone. Lob one over the customer's head on the patio: it misses them.
>

S9-HOUSE
Throw high at the house: an upstairs window breaks. Higher still: it lands on the roof,
clatters, and drops off the gutter in front of the house.
>

S9-HEAD
Tilt all the way up and tap throw: it comes straight back down on your head. OW, stars.
>

S9-FETCH
With the dog at your feet, throw the ball: it sails past the dog, and the dog fetches it.
>

S9-HEDGEHOG
Grab a hedgehog bare-handed: it's in your hands for a moment before the OW.
>

S9-KO
Throw stones at critters. Mostly they're knocked out: on their side with stars, then they
get up and run. Now and then one dies: an intact body, belly up, no splat. Does a body
read as dead, and different from knocked out?
>

S9-BODY
Pick a body up. Lob it over the fence, or put it in the truck, or mow it (mulch, no
splat). Kill one while the customer is inside and hide it: nothing is ever said. Leave
one lying in view: they come out and still say nothing, but after you leave the summary
screen lists "A dead hedgehog on the lawn".
>

S9-GRIT
Mow some stones: the ones that don't fly off leave a puff of grey grit.
>

S9-HOSE
The hose is orange, on a brass tap at the side of the house. Pick it up by the end and
drag it: it follows, and stops you at its length. Grab it near the tap: you can't take it
as far. Mow it: cut there, the far end shredded, a puddle, "My HOSE!".
>


================================================================================
LOAD 2 of 3: TALKING AND THE BOARD      (about 8 min, two jobs)
================================================================================

S9-TALK
Hop off and walk up to the customer: "[E] talk to ...". "How am I doing?": their answer,
what they said at the briefing, and a "Wants it: ..." line. The pause menu shows that too.
Mow, then ask to be paid there, face to face. The truck only offers leaving. Try it while
they're indoors: they come out to the door.
>

S9-BOARD
After a messy job: the board's header is on two rows and nothing runs off the right side.
On the summary screen before it, the counts scroll along as a ticker. Do more of something than your previous record (windows,
flowers) and it says PERSONAL WORST (or BEST for dogs walked home, balls fetched).
>

S9-CAR
Bump the customer's car slowly: a dent bill, no WANTED. Ram it at full speed on the petrol
or ride-on: dent bill and WANTED.
>


================================================================================
LOAD 3 of 3: CHURCHYARD, TREES, PAD      (about 6 min)
================================================================================

S9-VICAR
[4] down to a "Churchyard" ad: it says "Ring the vicarage". The customer is "Reverend ...",
black shirt and white collar. Squash something: "May God forgive you" or "Lord have mercy!".
>

S9-TREES
Walk under a tree so its canopy fades: the trunk forks into branches, no flat cut.
>

S9-PAD
(If a pad is handy.) On the mower: right trigger drives, left trigger reverses, the stick
steers.
>


================================================================================
LOAD 4 of 6: THE NEW STREETS      (about 12 min, use [2] and [4] to move reputation)
================================================================================

S10-TERRACE
Reputation under 40 ([4] a few times): a "Long garden, terraced house." ad. The house is
at the road end with a gravel passage down its side, a long thin garden behind, and the
customer on a patio out the back. Terraces either side, sometimes a council playground.
Mow it: do the long garden and the walk back for fuel feel fair against their patience?
>

S10-SEMI
Reputation 40 to 70: the house stands forward with its garage and drive, a back garden
behind, the patio out back, the neighbours set forward too. Stand just behind the roof's
ridge: the house fades.
>

S10-L
Reputation 70 or more ([2] a few times): next door's back corner is cut into the garden,
fenced on two sides, by the house. You can't get in. A stone thrown into it is gone.
>


================================================================================
LOAD 5 of 6: THE MANOR AND THE CHURCHYARD      (about 12 min)
================================================================================

S10-MANOR
Reputation 75 or more, a "Country estate" ad. First look: does it read as a stately
home, not a McMansion? Railings and shut gates on the road, the approach up the middle,
the truck at a side gate by the coach house, a loop drive or forecourt, box-edged beds,
topiary in pairs, a back lawn, parkland with no neighbours.
>

S10-TOPIARY
Knock into a topiary: a chunk comes out, -$60, "My TOPIARY!". Knock it again: nothing more.
>

S10-HAHA
Throw a stone low at a side or back edge of the manor: it drops into the ditch and is
gone, no bounce. Low at the road railings: it bounces back.
>

S10-CHURCH
Reputation under 20, a churchyard: the church to one side, walled off behind, a lychgate
over the path with the truck at it, headstones leaning more towards the edges.
>


================================================================================
LOAD 6 of 6: WHO SEES WHAT      (about 12 min, any garden)
================================================================================

S10-SIGHT
With the customer on the patio, go behind the house, the garage, the car or the truck:
their portrait greys. Step back into view: it's normal again.
>

S10-CONE
When they're at a window: a faint lit wedge on the ground in front of it. Outside the
wedge their portrait greys. Is the wedge easy to see, without being loud?
>

S10-UNSEEN
While their portrait is grey, flatten some flowers and mow a gnome: no reaction. They
step outside: still nothing. Finish and leave: a summary screen lists "After you left,
the customer noticed:" with the gnome and the flowers, a reputation for each and a total.
>

S10-CARRY
Kill a critter while unseen, pick up the body and walk into their sight: they react at
once.
>

S10-SUMMARY
After any job, the summary screen: the job's card, the ticker, the noticed list (if
anything), then "Back to the board". Does the card's "Reputation" line read as at odds with
the list's "all told" total?
>
