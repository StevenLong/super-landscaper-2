S6-WINDOW
Throw a stone at a ground-floor window: the glass smashes where the window is.
>It does, but I have a few thoughts on stones and throwing in general here.

If a stone hits the house (not the window), it just vanishes. I think stones should have a bit more persistence: they should bounce off the wall and land on the ground.

Also, the actual throwing makes it quite difficult and kind of unsatisfying to use because it's a fixed power or distance that you throw at. It would be better if the throw button charged the throw distance or power (or whatever), and maybe we could also have a guideline to show the stone's trajectory as you increase in power.

I also think once there are other objects in the game, things you can hold should maybe always be throwable. I'm including stuff like the petrol can in that, and there may be a situation where that gets thrown through a window.
