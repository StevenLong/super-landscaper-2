PLAY CHECKS OWED  (24 checks, 3 loads, about 50 minutes)

Answer on the line that starts with ">". Leave it blank if you didn't try that one.
Write whatever you like: "yes", "no, it did X", "didn't try". Prose is fine.
Cheats (debug builds): on the board [1] adds $500, [2] adds 20 rep, [4] takes 20 rep off,
[3] skips to the week's end (payday); in a job [0] finishes it perfectly.


================================================================================
LOAD 1 of 3: A JOB ON FOOT      (about 20 min, a garden with a dog and a hose is best)
================================================================================

S11-ARC
Pick up a stone and hold throw. The whole flight shows as a dotted arc with a faint
shadow, and the ring sits where it first hits: flat on the lawn, or standing up on the
house wall. Put the ring on an upstairs window and let go: does it hit that window? Try
the roof too. Is the arc easy to read?
>

S11-HOLE
Break a window, then throw another stone at the same window: a thud, no second bill.
>

S11-OVER
Lob a stone high over the side fence, then a body: they fly on and lie next door, where
you can see them but not reach them. Lob a live critter over: it wanders off.
>

S11-KOCARRY
Knock a hedgehog out with a stone and pick it up bare-handed: OW, but when it drops it's
still out cold. Knock a squirrel out, carry it a while: no bite. Throw a live squirrel
hard at the house wall: it's knocked out.
>

S11-CARRY
(Rolled on from S10-CARRY.) A hand-thrown stone kills about one critter in five. Kill one
while the customer can't see you, pick up the body and walk into their sight: they react
at once.
>

S11-HEAP
Put a gnome down next to a body and near the hose's end. White brackets show what [E]
would take, and the hint names it. Turn to face each one: the brackets move to it.
>

S11-HOSE
The hose is green, on a reel below a chunkier tap. Take its end and walk off: it pays off
the reel, bends round, never kinks or folds. Let go, walk to the reel, [E]: it winds in.
[E] again at the reel pulls it out again. Mow it: cut, and shorter from then on.
>

S11-LEAD
Walk into the loose dog: nothing happens. Throw its ball: it fetches; throw again: it
fetches again. [E] by the dog puts the lead on; [E] again picks it up.
>

S11-DOOR
While the customer's indoors, walk up to the front door: "knock on the door". Knock: a
pause, the door opens, they're standing in it, then you talk. Close the chat: they step
out onto the patio and the door shuts. Later, when they go back in off screen, do you
hear the door?
>

S11-DOGHOME
Walk the dog home while its owner's indoors: they come out pleased.
>

S11-HOUSE
The house front is plain cream with faint courses, no slanted brown grid (ask your partner).
A stone on the wall while they're inside: just a thud, no voice.
>

S11-MOTOR
Hop off the petrol mower with it running and walk away: its motor gets quieter and moves
to one side.
>


================================================================================
LOAD 2 of 3: THE MOWERS      (about 15 min; [1] on the board for money, buy petrol and ride-on)
================================================================================

S11-PUSH
The push mower walks at a slower pace. Hold [R] (pad RB) to push flat out: full speed, and
the stamina bar drains faster. Does the walking pace feel right against the customer's
patience?
>

S11-RIPCORD
The petrol mower won't go until you start it: hold W (pad RT), a white marker sweeps a
meter, let go in the green. Too early or too late: a cough and a second lost. Does it
feel good, or fiddly by the third job?
>

S11-STALL
Knock the petrol mower about till its condition is under 40%, then bump into things: now
and then it stalls and wants the cord again.
>

S11-GEARS
The ride-on: [R] up a gear, [C] down (pad RB, LB); the hint shows the gear. Each gear is
faster; top gear turns wide. Does it feel good to drive?
>

S11-RUNOVER
Run over hedgehogs with the push mower: mostly they're knocked out and flung aside, not
splatted. With the ride-on: always a splat.
>

S11-PAD
(Pad.) A gets you back on the mower, B gets off, X throws, B backs out of the pause menu.
On the mower the triggers drive and the stick only steers.
>

S11-BOARD
On the board, buy something: the cursor stays on that row instead of jumping to the top.
>


================================================================================
LOAD 3 of 3: STREETS, VENUES, THE SUMMARY      (about 15 min, use [2] and [4])
================================================================================

S11-SPAWN
An L plot (reputation 70 or more) and a churchyard (under 20): critters walk in from
outside the garden, never stuck spinning in a wall or a headstone. A hedge rustles as one
pushes through it; no leaves on walls or fences. Squirrels come down trees, never rocks.
>

S11-MANOR
The manor's roof ends are square: no corners of grass you can't reach. Walk round to the
back lawn: the house fades only when you're behind a chimney.
>

S11-HAHA
The manor's ha-ha now: stone coping at the lawn's edge, a shadowed drop, the bank rising
to the park. Does it read as a sunken wall? A stone thrown low goes in and lies there.
>

S11-SUMMARY
After a messy job, the summary has two panels: Money (each line, then Net) and Reputation
(each line, what they noticed, then All told, and how much shows in your standing yet).
Do the totals read clearly? A cut hose or the dog walked home shows as an event, with no
PERSONAL WORST.
>
