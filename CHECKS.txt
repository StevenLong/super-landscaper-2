LOAD 1 of 3: A JOB ON FOOT      (about 20 min, a squirrel hater with a hose is best)
================================================================================

S12-HATER
For a customer who wants squirrels gone: throw a squirrel hard into the fence or the house
wall while they watch. No WANTED. Throw one at their car: WANTED.
>

S12-UNSEEN
While the customer is inside, throw a hedgehog into the fence. No WANTED pops up.
>

S12-EVICT
Throw a squirrel over the fence while they watch. A squirrel hater is glad ("And STAY
out!"); a customer who likes wildlife isn't.
>

S12-BODY
Kill a critter with a stone, pick up the body and carry it into their sight: they react.
Put it down and pick it up again in view: no second reaction. Bare-handed, a dead hedgehog
still pricks you (OW).
>

S12-BODYHIT
Throw a dead hedgehog at a live one: the live one is hit, and the body lands beside it.
>

S12-HOSE
Mow through the middle of the hose. The far piece stays on the lawn: pick it up by either
end and drag it out of the way.
>

S12-WALLDROP
Throw a stone high at the house wall. It falls from where it hit rather than snapping to
the ground. Does the drop look right?
>

S12-PORTRAIT
Walk past tall things so the customer loses sight of you. The portrait eases to grey (no
flicker) with UNSEEN across it. Does it read clearly now?
>

S12-DONE
Get paid, then talk to them again: no "How am I doing?".
>

S12-BANNERS
Get fired and have the police called by the same act (a stone at the customer, say): the
two banners show one after the other, not on top of each other.
>
