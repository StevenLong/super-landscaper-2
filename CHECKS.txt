Answer on the line that starts with ">". Leave it blank if you didn't try that one.
Write whatever you like: "yes", "no, it did X", "didn't try". Prose is fine.
Cheats (debug builds): on the board [1] adds $500, [2] adds 20 rep; in a job [0] finishes it perfectly.


================================================================================
LOAD 1 of 3: A JOB, PAD IN HAND      (about 6 min, a gardener job is best)
================================================================================

S5-POP
Listen to the first second of a job as it loads. Is the pop gone?
>

S5-SCREAM
Mow straight through a flower bed. The customer screams once for the burst, not once per
flower, and speaks up again if you keep at it a couple of seconds later.
>

S5-TALK
Any customer line: the words come out slower, and the mouth visibly opens and shuts.
>

S5-PROMPTS
Press anything on the pad: the hints switch to (A) / (X) / (B). Touch the keyboard: back to
[E] / [F] / [Q]. The first job's briefing and the title's help follow it too.
>


================================================================================
LOAD 2 of 3: STONES      (about 4 min, [2] a few times for a lawn with a pond)
================================================================================

S5-POND-EDGE
Throw a stone at the very edge of the pond, onto the rim stones. A smaller splash, and the
rings stay on the water.
>

S5-TREE-SHAKE
Throw a stone at a tree trunk: the whole canopy sways and settles, and the leaves fall from
the canopy, not from a tuft at the trunk.
>


================================================================================
LOAD 3 of 3: THE 3/4 LOOK      (about 8 min, any job; [1] for cash to try other mowers)
================================================================================

S6-LOOK
Look around the whole garden (hold the look key too). Does it read as one 3/4 world: the
house, garage, fences or hedges, truck, trees and mower? Say what jars, if anything.
>

S6-FACINGS
Drive the mower round in a slow full circle. It turns through 8 facings with the operator
always behind the handle, and no step between facings jars. Same with another mower if you
can buy one.
>

S6-DEPTH
Drive behind a tree trunk and into the strip of lawn behind the garage: you go behind them.
Come back out in front: you're drawn over them.
>

S6-HEDGE
Mow along the fence or hedge by the road: it fades while you're behind it, so you can still
see the lawn's edge.
>

S6-FOOT
Hop off and walk about carrying a stone, then let a hedgehog and a squirrel cross. You, the
stone and the critters face where you're going; the stone goes behind you as you walk away.
>

S6-WINDOW
Throw a stone at a ground-floor window: the glass smashes where the window is.
>
