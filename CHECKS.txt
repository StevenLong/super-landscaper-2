LOAD 1 of 5: TITLE AND BOARD      (about 2 min)
================================================================================

S1-ATTRACT
The title's background mower stripes the lawn behind the menu; the text stays readable.
>

S1-FONT
The pixel font reads well everywhere (title, board, HUD, panels); nothing is clipped.
>

S1-BOARD
Offers, shop and header fit; F9 adds money; buying and using mowers updates the board.
>

S1-MENU-AUDIO
Menu music plays; the button move/select blips aren't annoying.

================================================================================
LOAD 2 of 5: A FIRST JOB, PUSH MOWER      (about 8 min)
================================================================================

S1-BRIEF
The briefing shows the customer, their hints, and (first job only) the controls.
>

S1-PUSH
Push mower: speed, cut width, stamina (PEP/ZZZ gauge) and resting feel right; exhausted it crawls but still cuts.
>

S1-STRIPES
Mowing up/right leaves light bands, down/left dark; mowing over a patch again restripes it.
>

S1-CRITTERS
Hedgehogs and squirrels read at a glance; a squash reads (splat, squeak, shake, face, speech).
>

S1-FACE
The corner face follows their mood and reacts to events; the patio customer watches you and hops.
>

S1-NAG
Past their patience they nag ("Are you nearly done?"). Is that enough of a clue to the hidden time?
>

S1-HANDIN
At the truck: sent back if far short; once paid you can hang about; the pay screen makes sense.
>

S1-WINNABLE
Is a push-mower job winnable? (The balance bot takes ~4 min to reach 70% of a small lawn.)
>


================================================================================
LOAD 3 of 5: A PETROL MOWER JOB      (about 6 min)
================================================================================

S1-ENGINE
Engine note rises with speed; low-fuel beep; glug while refuelling at the truck.
>

S1-TANK
The 60s tank and the refuel trips feel like a real trade against the push mower.
>

S1-CONDITION
OK/BUST gauge: head-on hits hurt, scraping along walls doesn't; parking at the truck repairs.
>

S1-STONES
Mow over a stone: clonk, damage, usually it flies. Seen it hit a window (-$40), the wall, the truck (-$20), wildlife, the customer?
>

S1-ONFOOT
[F] hop off: the camera follows you, the parked mower shows empty; carry a stone to the truck [E]; fetch the fuel can from the truck menu and pour it [E].
>

S1-DOG
On dog jobs the dog gets out and bounds about; walking it home earns thanks; bowling it over is a disaster.
>

S1-TREES
Can you mow tight to a trunk? Canopies hide the grass under them: fair or annoying?
>


================================================================================
LOAD 4 of 5: MISBEHAVING      (about 5 min)
================================================================================

S1-MISCHIEF
After being paid, squash/trample/smash something: "Rep -N" pops, and the result screen charges it.
>

S1-KO
Run the customer over on the patio: knocked flat, KO face, only "Leave quietly", no pay.
>

S1-WANTED
The board then shows WANTED; a later job may open with ARRESTED and end the run.
>

S1-FIRED
Getting fired (a gardener's flowers, or mood hitting zero) ends the job with the buzzer.
>

Bigger lawns unlock as reputation rises; the board shrinks as it falls; bankruptcy at zero.
