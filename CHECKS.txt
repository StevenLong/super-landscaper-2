PLAY CHECKS OWED  (15 checks, 4 loads, about 20 minutes)

Answer on the line that starts with ">". Leave it blank if you didn't try that one.
Write whatever you like: "yes", "no, it did X", "didn't try". Prose is fine.
Cheats (debug builds): on the board [1] adds $500, [2] adds 20 rep; in a job [0] finishes it perfectly.


================================================================================
LOAD 1 of 4: TITLE, BOARD AND SOUND      (about 4 min)
================================================================================

S4-SETTINGS
The title has Music and Sound buttons. They flip, relabel, and the setting holds in a job.
>

S4-PAD-MENUS
Gamepad: A presses buttons in every menu (title, board, briefing, truck, pause).
>

S4-ADS
The board reads as newspaper classifieds, no pictures. Can you tell what each customer is
about from the ad alone?
>

S4-AUDIO
Listen through the menu blips (now lower and softer) and a job's sounds and music. Name the
worst offenders, for the SNES-style audio pass (NOTES 45).
>


================================================================================
LOAD 2 of 4: A JOB ON THE STREET      (about 6 min)
================================================================================

S4-STREET
You start at the end of the drive by your truck, parked at the kerb. Drive up the drive and
back out to the truck. Next door's lawns and the road are there to see but not reach. Over a
few jobs the garage turns up on both sides of the house. Anything feel off?
>

S4-TREES
Trees now have a visible trunk under a raised canopy, and the canopy fades while you're under
or beside it. Do they read as trees? Only the trunk is solid now.
>

S4-VIEW
Feel check (NOTES 44): the trees and house are 3/4 view, the mower, truck and player are
straight top-down. Does it look like a mismatch?
>

S4-TELLS
Before a critter comes out, its hedge, fence or tree jiggles and drops a few leaves.
Noticeable without being loud?
>

S4-SQUASH
Run over a critter: the splat stays for the rest of the job, and the mower trails red for a
short way after.
>

S4-DOG
Catch the loose dog on foot: a red lead to your hand, "On the lead!", and hints that say what
to do.
>


================================================================================
LOAD 3 of 4: STONES      (about 5 min, [2] a few times for a lawn with a pond)
================================================================================

S4-SPLASH
Throw a stone into the pond: it flies over the edge, lands in the water and splashes (rings,
spray, a splash sound, no glug).
>

S4-THROWS
Throw stones at: a window (it stays smashed), your own parked mower (it dents), a tree
(leaves fall), a flower bed (a flower or two flattens), a critter (easier to hit than before).
>

S4-TALLY
After the job, the board's Last job panel lists "Also counted: ..." for what you did. File for
bankruptcy (or run out of rep) to see the whole run's tally. A pleasant surprise?
>


================================================================================
LOAD 4 of 4: GETTING FIRED      (about 3 min, needs a gardener job)
================================================================================

S4-FIRED
Trample the gardener's flowers until they fire you: their line types out under the portrait
while the mouth moves, "Rep -18" pops beside it, and a big YOU'RE FIRED banner shows above you
without stopping play. Does it read as a breakpoint now?
>

S4-TALKING
Any customer line (a nag, a squashed hedgehog): the mouth moves while the words come out,
then stops.
>
