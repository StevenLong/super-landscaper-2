PLAY CHECKS OWED  (21 checks, 5 loads, about 35 minutes)

Answer on the line that starts with ">". Leave it blank if you didn't try that one.
Write whatever you like: "yes", "no, it did X", "didn't try". Prose is fine.
Cheats (debug builds): on the board [1] adds $500, [2] adds 20 rep, [4] takes 20 rep off,
[3] skips to the week's end (payday); in a job [0] finishes it perfectly.


================================================================================
LOAD 1 of 5: A JOB ON FOOT      (about 9 min, a garden with a dog is best)
================================================================================

S8-FOOT
Hop off and walk every direction, west especially. The walker never draws upside down and
faces the way you walk.
>

S8-THROW
Pick up a stone and HOLD throw. You stand still, left/right swing the aim, a white ring
slides out as power builds and the camera leads toward it. Let go: it lands on the ring.
Hold again and press hop: cancelled, stone still in hand. Does the aim speed feel right?
>

S8-BOUNCE
Throw stones at the house wall, the car and your truck. Each bounces back and lands on the
lawn instead of vanishing.
>

S8-PROPS
Find the gnome, flamingo, cone and hose on the lawn and mow each. Gnome: bits fly and the
customer shouts about it. Cone: sent flying. Hose: a blue puddle. Also grab the petrol can
at the truck, drop it on the lawn with E and mow it: a brown patch.
>

S8-FETCH
With the dog out, throw the tennis ball: the dog runs for it and drops it at your feet.
Then mow the ball near the dog: it sits under a little rain cloud.
>

S8-CRITTERS
Press E on a hedgehog bare-handed: OW, stars, can't move for a moment. Buy gardening gloves
([1] for money), pick one up and throw it. Hold a squirrel: it bites free after a few
seconds. Pick up the dog and carry it to the patio.
>

S8-ROCKS
Some gardens have grey boulders: solid to the mower, stones clonk off them.
>


================================================================================
LOAD 2 of 5: CRIME      (about 7 min, two jobs)
================================================================================

S8-POLICE
Knock the customer out. They stay facing the way they fell. POLICE CALLED, a flashing
countdown top centre, a siren getting louder. Drive off in time: the board shows WANTED.
>

S8-RIFLE
Knock one out again, get off, HOLD E over them: "Rifling... $n" counts up. Then let the
countdown run out: NICKED, a fine, a night in the cells; the board's rundown shows the fine
and "next job lost", and the job counter skips one.
>

S8-CAR
Ram the customer's car: "My CAR!", a dent bill; harder on a ride-on at full speed.
>

S8-HITBOX
The car and your mower bump into things where their sprites are, not short of them
(the car used to stop you well south of its bumper).
>


================================================================================
LOAD 3 of 5: THE CUSTOMER      (about 7 min, the "quick mow" busy customer is best)
================================================================================

S8-WATCH
Dawdle. Around three quarters of their patience the portrait glances at a watch (no
words), again later; the first nag comes with a sigh; later nags get sharper, the last a
final warning.
>

S8-INSIDE
They go indoors now and then: the portrait greys out. Squash a hedgehog while they're in:
no reaction until they come out, then "What happened to the hedgehog?!". At a window their
head shows at the glass and the portrait gets a pane; a stone through that window hits them.
>


================================================================================
LOAD 4 of 5: A SEASON      (about 6 min, cheats)
================================================================================

S8-SEASON
The board header shows the week, the job and what's owed Friday. Press [3]: FRIDAY. PAYDAY.
Short of cash, sell buttons appear, or "Let them take it" loses your dearest kit. Paid,
back to the classifieds. Kit can also be sold from the board any time.
>

S8-WIN
[1] a few times and [3] through all four Fridays: SEASON WON with the tally.
>

S8-DREGS
New run, [4] until reputation hits bottom: one grumpy half-pay job, no bankruptcy button.
>


================================================================================
LOAD 5 of 5: VENUES AND LOOKS      (about 6 min)
================================================================================

S8-MANSION
[2] to the top band until a "Country estate" ad shows. A big pale house with a portico, a
gravel loop round a flower island, urns on the lawn (mowing one costs $120).
>

S8-CHURCH
[4] down under "Poor" until a "Churchyard" ad shows. A church, the vicar at the door, rows
of headstones (solid) with flowers laid at some.
>

S8-TREES-CHIMNEY-GARAGE
Tree trunks meet the ground in a rounded foot with a shadow; the chimney stands up through
the roof; the garage looks deep enough for the car.
>

S8-FENCES
The sides and road-side run are one style and join at the corners; the back run goes the
full width (a hedge shows over the roof); next door's garage and drive stay off your fence.
>

S8-BEDS-POND
Beds are ovals, kidneys and long beans with flowers standing up, sunk slightly into the
lawn. The pond's rim stones stand up and the water sits below a dark bank. Next door has
paths and beds too.
>
